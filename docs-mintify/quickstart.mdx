## Get Started in 5 Minutes

Yona brings privacy to Solana through zero-knowledge proofs. Shield your tokens, swap anonymously, and withdraw to any address.

### 1. Connect Your Wallet

Visit the Yona app and connect your Solana wallet:
- Phantom, Backpack, Solflare, or any Solana wallet
- No special permissions required
- Standard transaction signing only

### 2. Shield Your First Tokens

Move tokens into the private pool:

1. Select a token (SOL, USDC, USDT, or ZEC)
2. Enter amount (start with a small test amount)
3. Click "Shield Tokens"
4. Sign the transaction
5. **Important:** Download and save your encrypted backup

Your tokens are now private and anonymous.

### 3. Perform Private Operations

**Private Swap:**
- Exchange tokens anonymously
- Market rates via Jupiter
- No transaction linkages

**View Balance:**
- Check your private balance
- Only visible to you
- Calculated from encrypted UTXOs

### 4. Withdraw Anytime

Unshield tokens to any address:

1. Select token and amount
2. Enter recipient address (yours or anyone's)
3. Wait for proof generation (10-30 seconds)
4. Sign and submit transaction

No linkage to your original deposit.

## Security First

**Always Remember:**
- Backup your encrypted wallet data
- Store backups in multiple secure locations
- Test with small amounts first
- Never share your backup file

**Privacy Tips:**
- Use different withdrawal addresses
- Avoid immediate deposit-withdraw patterns
- Consider using the relayer for IP privacy
- Mix amounts to prevent correlation
